How breeding works in this build

  • P_INCENSE_BREEDINGBaby Pokemon (Wynaut, Azurill, Budew, Chingling, Bonsly, Mime Jr., Happiny, Munchlax, Mantyke) breed normally - no Incense needed.
  • P_EGG_HATCH_LEVELEggs hatch at level 1.
  • P_NIDORAN_M_DITTO_BREEDNidoran-M and Volbeat bred with Ditto can produce female offspring (Nidoran-F / Illumise).
  • P_BALL_INHERITINGThe egg inherits a parent's Poke Ball; the father can pass it down too, as long as he's the same species as the mother.
  • P_TM_INHERITANCEFathers do not pass down TM moves.
  • P_MOTHER_EGG_MOVE_INHERITANCEEither parent can pass down Egg Moves.
  • P_NATURE_INHERITANCEA parent holding an Everstone always passes down its Nature.
  • P_ABILITY_INHERITANCE80% chance to pass down a regular Ability, 60% for a Hidden Ability. Either parent can pass it, including when bred with Ditto.
  • P_EGG_MOVE_TRANSFERA Pokemon holding a Mirror Herb in the Day Care learns Egg Moves from the other Pokemon, regardless of species.
  • P_EGG_CYCLE_LENGTHEgg cycles use the original (longer) step counts.
  • P_UPDATED_EGG_GROUPSGen 8's egg group additions are applied.
  • P_SCATTERBUG_LINE_FORM_BREEDThe Scatterbug/Spewpa/Vivillon line always breeds into the Fancy pattern.
