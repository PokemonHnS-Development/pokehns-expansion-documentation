Pokéblocks are berries blended into a block. In Heart & Soul they do more than raise contest stats: leave one on a Safari Zone feeder and it decides the ability, the IVs and the nature of everything you catch there. Every number on this page is worked out with the game's own arithmetic. Jump to the feeder rules.

The Safari Zone Pokéblock feeder

How the feeder works

The Safari Zone has feeders you can stand in front of and leave a Pokéblock on. While the block is out it changes every wild Pokémon the area gives you:

  • Every Pokémon comes with its Hidden Ability, whichever block you used.
  • The block's colour decides which 3 IVs come out perfect - a Gold block perfects 5.
  • 80% of the time the nature is one that likes the block's flavors; the rest of the time it is random.
  • A block lasts 300 steps and then runs out. Up to 10 feeders can be going at once.
