GameplayHow do I enter the Ruins of Alph puzzle chambers?This is different than the vanilla games; it requires using the Pokemon for the respective puzzle.(For fossil Pokemon) Solve the puzzle room and collect the fossil when you drop down. (For Ho-Oh) Bring Ho-Oh as your first party member and interact with the sensor on the north wall.

OtherCan I save state reset for shinies/natures/EVs?Technically yes, but the main answer is no. You are reloading the same seed every time which means if you are encountering the Pokemon at the same time, you will be hitting the same few frames over and over. To save state reset, you would have to change up your actions slightly which can be slower and more inefficient. Soft resetting is easier and more effective.
