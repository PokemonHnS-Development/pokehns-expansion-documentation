ShinyWhat's the best way to shiny hunt Entei and Raikou?Entei and Raikou roam independently - both start roaming as soon as you awaken them in the Burned Tower, and neither gates the other. Their shininess and IVs are decided when they are created, so the way to re-roll them is to have them created again by beating the Kanto League.Both are created together at level 40 by the Burned Tower event, with shininess and IVs fixed at that moment.
Because the roll happens at creation, re-encountering the same roamer always gives the same result. Running away and tracking it down again will not re-roll it.
Defeating or catching a roamer takes it off the map, and it will not come back on its own.
Entering the Hall of Fame as Kanto Champion respawns them: the caught flags are cleared and Raikou and Entei are created fresh, with new IVs and shininess. The Kanto roamer is recreated at the same time. Beating the Kanto League again repeats this, so it is the repeatable way to hunt them.
Before that point, save ahead of the Burned Tower event and re-trigger it to re-roll.
You can track a roamer's current location from your Pokedex.

ItemsDoes the Exp Share S stack with the Exp Share key item?Yes. The Exp Share S splits the battle's EXP between its holder and the Pokemon that fought. The Exp Share key item generates additional EXP and shares it with the whole party.Example: you defeat a Pokemon worth 100 EXP. The Pokemon in the battle and the Pokemon holding the Exp Share S get 50 each. The rest of your party earns roughly 20 each, so with a full party the battle is worth about 200 EXP overall.
